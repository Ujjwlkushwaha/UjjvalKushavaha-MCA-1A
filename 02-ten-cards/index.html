Ten Cards in a Row

Mountain

A quiet place high above the clouds.

Golden sand and the sound of waves.

Forest

Tall trees and fresh morning air.

City

Bright lights and busy streets.

Desert

Endless dunes under a warm sun.

River

Water flowing gently through the valley.

Village

Small houses and a peaceful life.

Lake

A calm lake that mirrors the sky.

Bridge

Connecting two sides of the river.
